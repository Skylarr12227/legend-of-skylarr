CONTROLS

KEYBOARD

  • Arrows or WASD move
  • Z or Space A: talk, confirm, pick up
  • X, Shift or Esc B: back. Hold it to run
  • Enter START: open the menu

PHONE & TABLET

  • The D-pad moves. Slide your thumb between arrows to turn without lifting.
  • A talks and confirms, B goes back (hold to run), START opens the menu.
  • Turn your phone sideways for a bigger screen: the buttons move to the sides.

Talk to people, read signs, open chests and pick up items by facing them and pressing A.

GETTING STARTED

  • NEW GAME starts in LARKSPUR TOWN. Visit PROF. WREN's lab and choose your first partner.
  • Your starter is your only partner at first. The rest you catch, so take a few capsules on every route.
  • SAVE from the menu whenever you like. The game also remembers your options.
  • On this site, SIGN IN with Discord to keep your game online: it follows you to any device, and it's what lets you battle and trade with other players. Signed out, your game is kept in this browser only.

THE MENU

Press Enter (or START) out in the world.

  • TEAM up to six creatures. SUMMARY shows stats, moves and ability; SWITCH changes the order (the first one leads into battle); ITEM gives or takes a held item.
  • BAG five pockets: ITEMS, CAPSULES, HELD, KEY and DISKS. Use LEFT/RIGHT to change pocket.
  • DEX every creature you've seen and caught.
  • QUESTS side quests you've taken on, step by step.
  • SKYLARR your trainer card: badges, money, play time.
  • SAVE and OPTIONS (text speed, music, sound).

BATTLES

Walking through tall grass (and caves, and some water) starts wild battles. Trainers who spot you walk over and challenge you.

  • FIGHT pick one of up to four moves. Each move has a type, a power and limited uses (PP). Clinics refill PP.
  • BAG use a potion or throw a capsule. TEAM switch creatures (the foe gets a free hit). RUN escape a wild battle.
  • Types decide most battles. A super-effective move does double damage; some types resist or are immune. Moves that match their user's type hit harder too. See the type chart.
  • Status: BURN (less attack, loses HP), POISON (loses HP), SLEEP (can't move for a few turns), PARALYSIS (slow, sometimes can't move), CONFUSION (may hit itself). Clinics and cure items fix them.
  • Abilities every creature line has one, like KINDLING (PYRO moves power it up) or BEDROCK (survives one big hit at full HP). A banner shows when one kicks in.
  • Held items give one to a creature from TEAM or the BAG: cores boost a type, NUTRIENT GEL heals each turn, QUICK CHIP sometimes moves first, and more.

WEATHER

Some places, moves and abilities bring weather. It changes how hard types hit:

  • STORM VOLT x1.5, AQUA x1.2, PYRO x0.7
  • GALE AERO x1.5
  • SUNBURST PYRO x1.5, FLORA x1.2, AQUA x0.7
  • FOG SHADE x1.2, PSI x1.2

TYPE CHART

Find the attacking move's type along the top and the defender's type down the side. 2 = super effective, ½ = not very effective, 0 = no effect, blank = normal. A creature with two types takes both multipliers.

CATCHING

  • Weaken a wild creature first: the lower its HP, the better your odds. Sleep and paralysis help a lot.
  • Throw a capsule from the BAG in battle. If it breaks free, try again.
  • Rarer creatures are harder to catch. Some only appear at certain times of day, in certain weather or when fishing.
  • A team holds six. New catches go to the STORAGE BOX: use the PC in any clinic to swap them in.
  • CAPSULE A capsule for catching wild creatures.
  • SUPER CAPS. A better capsule with a higher catch rate.
  • HYPER CAPS. A top-grade capsule for tough creatures.
  • MASTER CAPS. PROF. WREN's prototype. It never fails.

GROWING UP

  • Creatures earn EXP. from battles and level up, learning new moves along the way. With four moves already, you choose one to forget.
  • Creatures don't evolve here: at milestone levels they transform into a bigger, upgraded form of themselves.
  • Some lines have forms, set by where or when they transform (the weather, the time of day, the kind of place).
  • Each content pack has a level cap: 60 in the first region, 75 in SKYBOUND. EXP. stops at the cap until the next pack opens.
  • A STUDY CHIP shares EXP. with a creature that sat the battle out, which helps raise new team members.

EXPLORING

  • CLINICS heal your whole team for free and have a STORAGE PC. MARTS sell potions, capsules and held items.
  • DAY & NIGHT the game has its own clock (a day lasts about 24 minutes). Different creatures come out at night, and some places change.
  • FISHING get a rod, face water and press A. When the ! appears, press A quickly to hook a bite.
  • ICE you slide until something stops you. Plan your route before you push off.
  • GYMS each leader gives a badge, and badges open the way onward.
  • GLIDING (SKYBOUND) step onto a swirling updraft with the GLIDER and it carries you to the next landing pad.
  • AIRSHIP (SKYBOUND) CAPTAIN JUNO flies between docks you've visited.

CONTENT PACKS

The story grows in packs: a new region, new creatures, new gyms and a higher level cap. Unlike starting a new game, you keep everything: your team, items, money, badges and dex.

  • PACK 1 level cap 60. Beat the champion, then finish the story on STARFALL PEAK.
  • PACK 2: SKYBOUND level cap 75. Opens once the pack before it is complete.

Finishing a pack earns a choice of three starter eggs for the next one. The egg hatches as a baby your team helps raise.

HIDDEN PLACES

Both regions hide optional areas that open up as you go: things you couldn't reach before become reachable later. Each ends in a battle with its keeper and a reward chest.

  • Look for cracked walls and cracked ice, warm updrafts in unexpected places, paths that only show at night, and locked hatches and doors. Locals often drop a hint.
  • Every reward chest holds a MOVE DISK: it teaches a strong move no creature learns by levelling, and it's never used up. Use it from the DISKS pocket in the BAG.

MOVE DISKS AND WHO CAN LEARN THEM

  • FORGE HAMMER MECH / PYRO / TERRA
  • SAP SIPHON FLORA / TOXIN / AERO
  • FROST NOVA FROST / AQUA
  • STARFALL PRISM / PSI / SHADE
  • STORM SURGE VOLT / AERO / AQUA
  • SECOND WIND any creature
  • GEM CRUSH TERRA / PRISM / MECH
  • CORRODE TOXIN / AQUA / SHADE

A rare forge serpent is said to sleep in the coals of an old foundry somewhere deep underground...

ONLINE (THIS SITE)

Sign in with Discord from the title screen, then choose VERSUS.

  • FIND A BATTLE join a player waiting in a public room. MAKE ROOM host one: pick ALL LV50 (fair for everyone) or REAL LEVELS, PUBLIC or PRIVATE. JOIN BY CODE enter a friend's 4-letter code.
  • Both players choose at the same time, with 60 seconds per turn. After the battle you can REMATCH.
  • QUICK CHAT in a battle, CHAT (where BAG usually is) sends a friendly phrase.
  • WATCH spectate a public battle live. PRACTICE battle the computer with your versus team (works signed out, too).
  • TRADE swap creatures with a friend using a trade room code. RECORDS two leaderboards, one for ALL LV50 and one for REAL LEVELS.

Your online creatures are checked by the save server, so every one is real and can't be copied.

TIPS & FAQ

  • Stuck? Talk to everyone in the last town, and check the QUESTS page. Gates usually need a badge or a story event.
  • Beat the champion? How do I get to SKYBOUND? Go through FROSTVALE's east gate and climb STARFALL PEAK to stop the REWIRE GANG's last plan. Then visit PROF. WREN's lab in LARKSPUR for a starter egg, and the airship captain JUNO will be waiting on the summit. MOM at home always knows the next step.
  • Team keeps fainting? Catch creatures that cover your starter's weaknesses, and train on the route before the next gym.
  • Lost? Fainting sends you back to the last clinic you used, with your team healed.
  • Running low on money? Beat trainers, and sell treasures you find at any MART.
  • Playing on a new device? Sign in on this site and your online game is there.
  • Found a bug or have an idea? Come say hi on Discord.
